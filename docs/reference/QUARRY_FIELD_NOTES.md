## 📑 ANALOG WORKFLOW & PAPER RECORD BREAKDOWN

### I. Core Quarry Workflow
Before the data ever hits a ledger, physical operations happen in the quarry.
- Sales flow sequentially: **Vehicle ➔ Party ➔ Material ➔ Quantity ➔ Rate ➔ Payment**.
- All material quantities are strictly measured and tracked in **CFT (Cubic Feet)**, not MT.
- Loading operators determine the load based on the vehicle's standard capacity or extra body size.
- Once loaded, the operator communicates dispatch details to the office clerk via verbal calls, radio, WhatsApp, or physical tokens.

### II. Physical Paper Usage
Staff currently rely on multiple separate paper books:

1. **Daily Sales Book**
   - Primary tracker for daily dispatches.
   - Columns: `Sl.No`, `Vehicle No`, `Party Name`, `Time`, `Material`, `Qty`, `Bank`, `Cash`, `Credit`.
   - Manual conditional sums at page bottoms for specific materials (40mm, 20mm, 12mm, 6mm, Dusts, MS, PS, GSB).
   - Color coding: Raw boulder purchases (*baler*) in **red ink**, standard sales in **blue ink**.

2. **Master Accounts Book (Overview Ledger)**
   - Tracks cash/bank flows, opening balances, operational expenses, closing balances.
   - Columns: `Ref/Type`, `Details`, `Amount Col 1`, `Amount Col 2`, `Amount Col 3`, `Amount Col 4`.
   - Cash sale entries must match Daily Sales Report totals.
   - Manual reconciliation blocks at bottom for truck trips and quantities for targeted accounts (e.g., "Balelu - 4mile").

3. **Credit Book**
   - Direct feeder into the Master Accounts Book.
   - Columns: `Date`, `Description`, `Qty (CFT)`, `Debit`, `Credit`, `Balance`.
   - Contains inline manual calculations combining multiple credit amounts.

4. **Dispatch Slips (Challans)**
   - Pre-printed delivery slips for vehicle & billing details.
   - Frequently misused as rough tally sheets; often left incomplete.
